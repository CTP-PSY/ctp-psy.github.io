  • Boom
    Boom Festival 2027: 30 years, sold out

    Boom marks thirty years since the first edition in 1997 with its 30th-anniversary gathering, 18–25 July 2027 at Boomland, Idanha-a-Nova, Portugal, under the theme Psychedelic Renaissance. All 40,000 tickets sold out in two hours on 13 October 2026. boomfestival.org

  • Ozora
    OZORA 2027: 23 July – 3 August, twelve days

    Europe's great psytrance gathering returns to the Dádpuszta valley near Ozora, Hungary — gates open Friday 23 July, opening ceremony Monday 26 July, twelve days to 3 August. Full-on and progressive at the core. Festival guide

  • Psy-Fi
    Psy-Fi on pause — no 2027 edition announced

    The Dutch festival's 2025 edition was cancelled because of low presales, and nothing has been announced for 2026 or 2027. Psy-Fi started in 2013 and grew to around 15,000; for now the scene waits. Status

Modular architecture — each layer does one job

  • Experience layer — this portal, a static site on GitHub Pages.
  • Syndication layer — psytrance news and festival dates gathered from the global scene and published here.
  • Internet radio layer — laut.fm streams powering the Radio Wall and the player.
  • Artist ecosystem layer — Bandcamp releases and artist pages — the curated releases above.
  • Membership layer — Club CTP identity, access and tiers.

Separating the layers keeps the portal lean, secure and cheap to run — and free to scale when the community does.
